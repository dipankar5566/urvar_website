# TrustBadges

A thin white strip of 5 outlined pills, each with a green dot: MSME Certified · Startup India · Organic · Eco-Friendly · Sustainable. It is centred, wraps on mobile, and has a hairline bottom border.

No props. Labels are localized. On the site it sits directly under the homepage `Hero`. Use it wherever a design needs a quick credibility line.

```jsx
<LangProvider>
  <Hero />
  <TrustBadges />
</LangProvider>
```
